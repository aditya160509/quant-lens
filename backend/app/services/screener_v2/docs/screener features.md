## FEATURE S5 — Screener Backtester with Point-in-Time Validation

**The money:** A self-backtesting screener not only screens stocks based on your criteria but automatically runs historical simulations to validate your strategy's edge — returning win rate, profit factor, average return, drawdown, and holding time. Tradomate's screener backtesting goes beyond a simple "pass/fail" result — it gives traders a rich picture of how a screener would have performed across time, stocks, and market conditions. The critical technical problem that most platforms ignore: AlphaScreener's backtest engine is engineered to be time-agnostic and to avoid lookahead bias — but almost no retail platform enforces point-in-time data discipline, meaning their backtests are contaminated with financial data that was not yet publicly available at the historical date being simulated. This platform enforces strict point-in-time discipline: every backtest uses only data that was available at the time of the simulated decision. No survivorship bias. No look-ahead. No exceptions.

**What it computes:** For any saved screen (from Feature S1, S2, or S3): replay the screen historically at user-defined frequency. At each historical date, apply the screen using only data available on that date. Record which stocks passed. Track performance of those stocks forward over the holding period. Produce full portfolio-level statistics.

| Input                  | Options / Range                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Backtest period        | Start and end date pickers; full history to 10 years                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Rebalancing frequency  | Weekly / monthly / quarterly / semi-annual / annual                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Portfolio construction | Long-only top decile / long-short (top decile long, bottom decile short) / top N stocks equal-weight (N user-set 5–100) / score-weighted (weight ∝ composite score)                                                    |
